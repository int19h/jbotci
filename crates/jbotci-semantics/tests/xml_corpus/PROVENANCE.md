# Frozen SFN-XML parity corpus

This directory vendors the authoritative SFN-XML adoption corpus from
`int19h/tersmu-dsl-research` commit
`b1ea3224f3b63250370218e132b8bb831fdeb499`.

- `*.frozen.json` are the 50 comparable canonical source graphs from
  `experiments/phase-a/battery-renders-smusni/fresh-json-v2`.
- `*.xml.txt` are the corresponding frozen outputs from
  `experiments/phase-a/battery-renders-xml`.

The corpus adds the minimized live-gate witnesses `b57`
(`da poi broda cu brode`, restricted explicit `EXISTS`) and `b58`
(`lo du'u makau cortu`, abstraction focus-parameter scope dependence), plus
`b59` (`mi djuno lo ka ce'u klama makau`) and `b60`
(`mi djica lo nu makau klama`), which pin the semantic first-visit binder
universe independently from lexical parameter-reference enclosure. Their
JSON was generated by the freshly built product binary with `--format json`;
the canonical product JSON/smusni behavior was not changed.

The pinned research directories also contain `b56-quote`, which the research
`README-xml.md` identifies as a separate XML check witness rather than a
PM-verified comparison golden. It is therefore intentionally excluded from
this 50-document product parity corpus.

Before these files were imported, a fresh run from the clean research checkout
at the pinned commit was performed:

```text
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 render_xml.py --check --output-dir <scratch>
diff -qr <scratch> experiments/phase-a/battery-renders-xml
```

The prototype's default and provenance checks passed all 51 research
documents, including the separate `b56-quote` witness. The selected
50-document relative-name/content `sha256sum` manifests for the research and
product XML directories both have aggregate SHA-256
`94ec55e551972c0872e0956e43588fd89d89ed0585d1deb180ad41bdfc13112a`.
All 50 product JSON files also compare byte-for-byte with the pinned research
sources. Product tests independently pin ordered name/content hashes
`a72dd0b4a72c877a48e9d0e214fcba782bb6a47548ce61c367dfd246f6f16aa7`
for JSON and
`839d0d8b8ddb3322d9448fe31685c14565ae767676e091995a1d9012cdb697c0`
for XML.

The files are frozen evidence. Update them only after a separately reviewed
notation decision and a fresh, pinned-oracle parity proof.

`BINDER_UNIVERSES.json` is the exact first-visit binder-universe projection
for the 50 XML corpus graphs and the 50 Phase B test graphs. It was generated
by the corrected mechanical traversal at the pinned research commit; its
SHA-256 is
`95e4bcfc308fca0ebbb3e92edee5c2f812f12fcac8391e275af8f23835a119f2`.
Raw `serde_json::Value` golden tests consume this projection instead of
attempting the lossy reconstruction `fixed => empty` and
`underspecified => mayDependOn`.
